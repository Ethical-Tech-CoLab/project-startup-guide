## 1. One-sentence pitch

> <Project Name> helps **<who>** do **<what>** so they can **<outcome>**.

Rules for this sentence:

- No jargon. A friend outside your field should understand it.
- One sentence. If you need two, the idea is still two ideas.

## 2. The problem

Describe the problem as it exists **today**, without mentioning your solution.

- Who has this problem?
- How do they cope with it right now (spreadsheet, group chat, paper, nothing)?
- What does the pain cost them — time, money, stress, missed opportunities?
- How do you know this is real? (conversations, personal experience, data, an article)
